    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about iLEAPP AI.

    What iOS versions does iLEAPP AI support?

    iLEAPP AI supports iOS versions 12 through 17, including the latest iOS 17.4 update. Our team regularly updates the tool to ensure compatibility with new iOS releases.

    Do I need an internet connection to use iLEAPP AI?

    The core functionality of iLEAPP AI works offline. However, some advanced AI features require an internet connection to access cloud-based language models. We also offer a fully offline version with local AI models for sensitive investigations.

    Is iLEAPP AI suitable for legal proceedings?

    Yes, iLEAPP AI is designed with legal requirements in mind. It maintains a detailed chain of custody, provides comprehensive audit logs, and generates court-ready reports. The tool has been used successfully in numerous legal cases.

    How does iLEAPP AI handle encrypted data?

    iLEAPP AI can analyze unencrypted portions of iOS backups without requiring the encryption password. For encrypted backups, you'll need the password to fully access the data. The tool includes advanced decryption capabilities for supported iOS artifacts.

    Can I integrate iLEAPP AI with other forensic tools?

    Yes, iLEAPP AI provides a comprehensive API that allows integration with other forensic tools. We also offer pre-built integrations with popular forensic platforms. Check our Integration Guide for detailed instructions.
